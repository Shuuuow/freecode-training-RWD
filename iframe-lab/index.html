Football Roles

Football is a simple game played by three main units on the pitch. The defense stays at the back to block the other team and protect the goal. In the center, the midfield acts as the bridge, passing the ball around and helping out both sides. Up front, the attack focuses on running forward and scoring goals. Together, these three groups work as a team to win the game.
